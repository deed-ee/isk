# Code of Conduct

ISK welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone
participating in project spaces or representing the project is expected to help
make participation harassment-free.

## Our standards

Treat people with respect, give and receive constructive feedback, and critique
ideas rather than people. Respect privacy and apologize when you cause harm.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwelcome sexual attention, and sharing
someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

A designated private reporting contact has not been published. Maintainers
should establish and publish one before asking people to submit reports.
Do not post sensitive conduct reports in public issues or pull requests.

Maintainers are responsible for reviewing reports and protecting reporters'
privacy. Depending on the harm and circumstances, they may issue a warning,
remove content, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently.
